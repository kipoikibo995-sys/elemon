## Việc 1 — Hư Ảnh, sheet đánh 4x6
- [ ]
- **Ảnh mẫu:** `assets/source/tinh-linh/HO-N1_viem-long.png` (bố cục 4x6 và cỡ nhân vật của một sheet đánh)
- **Lưu thành:** `BOSS_hu-anh.png`
- **Đích:** `../assets/source/tinh-linh/BOSS_hu-anh.png`
- **Lời nhắc:**
```
Create an image. Pixel art sprite sheet for a handheld pet fighting game. IMAGE 1 is a fighter sheet of the game: copy its layout, cell size, character size and art style exactly (24 frames, 4 columns x 6 rows, side view facing right, feet on the same line, chunky pixels, dark outline). Draw HU ANH, the final boss: a shadow spirit with no fixed shape, a body of swirling purple-black smoke shaped roughly like a hunched four-legged beast, two glowing white eyes, small cracked purple crystals floating around it, wisps of smoke for claws. Menacing but not gory, readable at small size, the same height as the character in IMAGE 1. Landscape canvas 1536x1024, flat solid magenta background #FF00FF everywhere (no floor, no shadow, no text, no numbers, no grid lines), everything fully inside its own cell, a clear magenta gap between cells. Row 1: idle x4 (the smoke breathing in and out). Row 2: punch x4 (wind up, lunge, a smoke claw slash, recover). Row 3: blast x4 (gathering a purple orb, the orb growing, firing a purple beam to the right, recover). Row 4: block (crystals shielding), hurt (recoiling), faint (smoke collapsing flat), victory (rising tall, eyes blazing). Row 5: eat, happy, tired, sick (the same shadow, small poses). Row 6: sleep, sleep (curled up dim), yawn, stretching up. SOLID opaque pixels only, no soft glow, no transparency.
```
